## 1. The direct answer: does a client need env keys?

**No.** There are two kinds of keys:

| Key | Whose | Where it lives | Customer sees it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase URL / keys, `META_APP_SECRET`, webhook verify token, Inngest keys, Gemini key | **Yours** (LeadOS, the platform) | Server env (Vercel later) | Never; this is correct |
| Customer's Meta token, ad accounts, Pages | Customer | Settings → Meta → Connect, encrypted in Vault | They paste it once |
| Customer's WhatsApp number + token | Customer | Settings → WhatsApp → Connect, encrypted in Vault | They paste it once |

So the per-client setup already happens in the app. What you did in `.env.local` was set up the *platform*, which every SaaS does once.

**But the connection experience is still developer-grade:**
- **Pasting a System User token is not something a real estate broker can do.** A real platform has a **"Continue with Facebook"** button (Facebook Login for Business) and WhatsApp **Embedded Signup** (a Meta pop-up where they pick or create their number). Both need Meta Business Verification, App Review and Tech Provider status for *your* Meta app. Those take weeks, so they should be submitted now.
- **The WhatsApp connect flow is not self-serve yet (bug, P0):**
  - After connecting, the page tells the customer to set a callback URL and verify token in "your Meta app". The customer doesn't have a Meta app or the verify token; those are yours.
  - The app should instead subscribe the customer's WhatsApp Business Account to our app automatically (`POST /{waba_id}/subscribed_apps`), the way it already does for Facebook Pages.
  - Right now the WABA id isn't even stored.
  - Without this, a customer's bot can send messages but never sees replies.
- **The WhatsApp connect form has never been used end to end.** Your number was connected by SQL because I can't type tokens into forms. The form exists, but nobody has clicked through it yet.
- **Templates have to be made by hand in WhatsApp Manager.** A customer won't know what `lead_welcome` with `{{1}}`/`{{2}}` means. The app can create all three templates through the API with one button, then show their approval status ("Pending / Approved / Rejected").

## 4. "Not just another AI tool": what actually saves customers money

What exists already is the plumbing: ads, leads and the bot. The things a broker will pay for are outcomes, and most are small additions on data you already have.

1. **Hot-lead alert to the salesperson's own WhatsApp** in seconds ("Rahul, 3 BHK, ₹1.5 Cr, wants a visit Saturday. Call now"). Speed-to-lead is the No. 1 driver of conversions in real estate.
2. **Cost per *qualified* lead and per site visit**, not just CPL. This shows which ads bring buyers rather than form-fillers. It is the number agencies can't get from Ads Manager.
3. **Wasted-spend alert**: an ad that spent ₹X with 0 leads, or only cold leads, for N days gets flagged, with an "open in Ads Manager" link.
4. **Daily 9 am WhatsApp/email summary**: yesterday's spend, leads, hot leads not yet called.
5. **Response-time tracking** per agent (lead in → first call/reply), so owners can see who's slow.
